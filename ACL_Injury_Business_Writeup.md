# ACL Injury Dataset Summary

This dataset was created as an Excel-ready CSV to support analysis of ACL injury risk by gender in a soccer context. The file can be opened directly in Microsoft Excel and filtered using Gender, Position, or Injury_Indicator.

## Data description
- Source: custom Excel-ready dataset based on collegiate athlete ACL risk factors and published injury-analysis structures
- Rows: 60
- Columns: 18
- Key variables: Age, Gender, Sport, Height_cm, Weight_kg, Position, Training_Intensity, Training_Hours_Per_Week, Recovery_Days_Per_Week, Match_Count_Per_Week, Rest_Between_Events_Days, Fatigue_Score, Performance_Score, Team_Contribution_Score, Load_Balance_Score, ACL_Risk_Score, Injury_Indicator

## Business relevance
This dataset is useful for sports medicine departments, athletic trainers, injury-prevention programs, and sports organizations. It can help estimate which athlete groups may need more ACL-prevention screening, deeper fatigue monitoring, or modified training loads.

## Sample business questions
1. What is the probability that a soccer athlete has an ACL injury indicator, and does that probability differ by gender?
2. How is the ACL risk score distributed across women and men?
3. Which training and recovery variables are most associated with higher ACL risk?

## Notes
This dataset is for educational and business-analysis purposes. It is not a replacement for a full clinical or sport-specific injury registry.
